# v1.7.5 (2026-11-04)

## Improved

- Revert the learning/demo/MVP verification exemption introduced in 1.7.3:
  classifying a task as a "demo scenario" is left to the model itself, which
  mints a self-signed waiver and invites lazy shortcuts; and the original
  rule (smallest check relevant to the change) already permits minimal
  verification for small tasks — the exemption only added a "may fall short"
  clause. Replaced with a positive statement: the right endpoint for a small
  task is minimal verification plus an honest level statement; level and
  uncovered boundaries are always required, no exemption scenario exists.
